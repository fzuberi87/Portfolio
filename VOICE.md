# Faiz Zuberi writing voice

Use this guide for portfolio copy, project descriptions, case studies, and direct replies written for Faiz.

## Voice

- Write in first person.
- Keep the tone direct, conversational, and specific.
- Use contractions throughout.
- Avoid a stiff, overly formal, or corporate register.

## Structure

- Open with a fact, a specific result, or a one-line comparison to the standard approach.
- Then pivot to a concrete named example from Faiz’s work, such as a project, client, or specific decision.
- When comparing against the expected approach, name the standard first, explain what changed, and let the reasoning make the case.
- When admitting a limitation or mistake, state it once plainly and move on.
- Keep paragraphs to 2 to 5 sentences.
- Pair one longer, comma-stacked sentence with one short sentence per paragraph, with the short sentence usually landing last.
- End with a short closing line that lands the takeaway or the ask.

## Mechanics

- Never use an em dash.
- Join thoughts with a comma or a spaced hyphen (` - `).
- Avoid slang, exclamation points used as emphasis, family or cultural references, food references, jargon, hedging language, and generic claims.
- Prefer a named project, client, number, or decision whenever one is available.
